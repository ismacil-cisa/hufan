Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to HUFAN. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a project inquiry through the HUFAN website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company or organization name
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your estimated project budget
  • Details about your project or requirements

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to project inquiries
  • Understand your project requirements
  • Evaluate whether our services are suitable for your needs
  • Communicate with you about your inquiry
  • Provide requested services or information

We do not use the information submitted through our project inquiry form to send unsolicited marketing emails.

3. How We Protect Your Information

HUFAN takes reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Information Sharing

HUFAN does not sell or rent your personal information. Information submitted through the project inquiry form is used for legitimate business communication and project evaluation.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect our legal rights.

5. Cookies

The HUFAN website does not currently use advertising cookies or user tracking systems for the purpose of building advertising profiles.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as hosting, content delivery, fonts, or other technical services necessary to operate the website.

These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

We retain project inquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage business communications, maintain appropriate business records, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided to us.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us at:

hufan.agency@gmail.com

9. Changes to This Policy

HUFAN may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how HUFAN handles information, please contact us.
